The app requests no permissions. No permission prompt appears, and there is no usage-description string.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no public user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Benday is a private year plate for one mark a day. You dip an ink, draw one stroke for today, and the year fills so you can see how the days felt. It is for anyone who wants a quiet daily mark on this device, not a feed or an account.

No credentials or demo account are needed. From a cold launch, a system launch screen appears first. Appearance is light only. A cold launch may then briefly show a spinner and the line "Opening the plate." If onboarding is not finished, three pages follow. A page control of three dots is visible and is not tappable. "Skip" is on every page and ends onboarding at once. Page one headline "One stroke for each day." body "The year matrix keeps a single mark so you can see how the days felt." button "Next". Page two headline "Dip, then lay the stroke." body "Choose an ink from the tray. The stroke commits when you lift." button "Next". Page three headline "Peel only today." body "A wrong lay clears the same day. Quiet streak counts the laid days you kept." button "Continue". "Skip" or "Continue" opens the year with the default tray. While that finishes, the main button shows a spinner and "Skip" is disabled. If onboarding is already finished, the year opens instead. The year title is "Lay today's stroke". Until any day in the year has been kept and today is still waiting for a dip, a full-screen empty plate covers the year with "The year is empty.", "The first stroke is yours.", and "Dip". "Dip" dips the first tray ink ("Chalk") and reveals the year. Tap a tray name to dip that ink, then draw in "Today's cell". It only accepts a stroke after you dip, and only for today. The stroke commits when you lift. If more than one stroke is drawn before you lift, only the last stroke is kept. "Peel today" clears only today's kept stroke and stays disabled until today is kept. A number sits next to the caption "Quiet streak". Pinch the year between one and three times size. Tapping a day in the year does not lay that day. Only today can take a stroke. "Analytics" opens the Analytics sheet. Swipe down to close. When at least one day is kept: "Quiet streak" with "Consecutive laid days", and "Ink mix" with one row per tray ink. When no day has been kept: "No ink has been laid.", "Dip an ink and lay today's stroke.", and "Lay today's stroke" which closes the sheet. "Settings" opens the Settings sheet titled "Tray ink names" with "These names sit on the ink tray under the year. Tap one to rename it." Each ink has "Rename this ink". The alert is "Rename {name}", "This word is the name on that tray ink.", "Cancel", and "Save name". A blank or spaces-only name is ignored. A name is kept up to 32 characters. "Export plate" opens the system share sheet with a copy of the plate. "Reset plate" shows "Reset the Benday plate?" and "This wipe removes every laid day on the plate." with "Cancel" and "Reset plate". Confirming wipes the year, dismisses the sheet, and returns you to onboarding. "Contact Benday" opens the support page outside the app.

Data stays on this device unless you tap "Export plate" and share the copy yourself. "Contact Benday" on Settings opens the support page.

Copy is English. The year, first weekday, month names, and numbers follow the device calendar and region.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. The app is a Lifestyle personal year plate and does not handle payments, accounts, medical records, or regulated goods.

The app does not show health, medical, or product-health information. The app is a personal log only and gives no recommendations.

The app does not scan barcodes or QR codes.
