Thank you for reviewing Spinochet. The build under review was retested on a clean device. No crash, dead end, empty screen or non-functioning feature could be reproduced.

Three behaviours are deliberate and can look stalled on a short pass.

1. Once a stroke is started in "Today's cell" and not yet lifted, tray wells go dim and ignore taps. The hint is "Finish this stroke before dipping another ink." Lift to commit the last stroke.
2. "Analytics" with no kept day shows "No ink has been laid." and "Dip an ink and lay today's stroke." Use "Lay today's stroke" to close the sheet and return to the year.
3. Confirming "Reset plate" on "Reset the Spinochet plate?" / "This wipe removes every laid day on the plate." wipes the year, dismisses the sheet, and runs onboarding again. That return is the wipe.

Full review path. No account, no login:
- Cold launch: system launch screen, light only. A spinner and "Opening the plate." may appear briefly.
- If onboarding is unfinished: three pages, dots visible and not tappable, "Skip" on every page. "One stroke for each day." then "Next"; "Dip, then lay the stroke." then "Next"; "Peel only today." then "Continue". "Skip" or "Continue" opens the year with the default tray. While that finishes, the main button shows a spinner and "Skip" is disabled. If onboarding is already finished, the year opens instead.
- Title "Lay today's stroke". No tabs. Until a day is kept and today still needs a dip, "The year is empty.", "The first stroke is yours.", and "Dip" cover the year. "Dip" dips "Chalk" and reveals the grid.
- Seven-column year, today ringed. Pinch one to three times size. A number sits next to "Quiet streak". Tap a tray name, then draw in "Today's cell"; the stroke commits when you lift. Only today accepts a stroke. Tapping another day only scrolls to it and updates the caption. "Peel today" stays dim until today is kept, then clears only that stroke.
- "Analytics" opens a sheet; swipe down to close. After a kept day: "Quiet streak" with "Consecutive laid days", and "Ink mix" with one row per tray ink.
- "Settings" opens a sheet titled "Tray ink names" with "These names sit on the ink tray under the year. Tap one to rename it." Each row has "Rename this ink". Alert title "Rename {name}", text "This word is the name on that tray ink.", "Cancel", "Save name". A blank name is ignored; extra characters past 32 are dropped. "Export plate" opens the system share sheet. "Contact Spinochet" opens the support page outside the app.

The tray always starts with "Chalk", "Clay", "Ember", "Moss", "Tide", "Dusk", "Linen", "Copper", "Sage", "Plum", "Sand", and "Soot". The year starts with no laid days. Laid days, today's dipped ink, a stroke started but not lifted, renamed inks, and finished onboarding come back on the next launch. "Reset plate" clears the year and onboarding.

Technical facts: no accounts, no in-app purchases, no ads, no tracking analytics, no permissions requested. No usage-description string, no public user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. The "Analytics" control is a local count of the plate only. Data stays on this device unless you tap "Export plate" and share the copy. Copy is English. The year, first weekday, month names, and numbers follow the device calendar and region. Portrait only, iPhone and iPad, light appearance, minimum iOS 17.0.
